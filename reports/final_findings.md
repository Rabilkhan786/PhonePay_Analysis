# Final findings

## Decision

Prioritize field validation in West Godavari and Sangareddy, followed by Dr BR Ambedkar Konaseema, East Godavari, Sri Potti Sriramulu Nellore, and Alluri Sitharama Raju. These districts combine a meaningful registered-user base, positive demand growth, comparatively low registered-merchant penetration, and strong activity per registered merchant. They also remain in the top ten when transaction intensity is removed from the score.

## Evidence

- Q2 2026 recorded **38.66 billion transactions**, **₹45.50 trillion** in value, **711.65 million registered users**, and **50.71 million registered merchants** in the mapped PhonePe ecosystem.
- 680 of 783 districts met the minimum scale and history rules for comparable scoring.
- Every default top-ten district recorded positive YoY transaction growth in each of the latest four quarters.
- Equal weights retain **9 of 10** districts. Removing transaction intensity retains **6 of 10**.
- The eligible-district median is **15.52 registered users per registered merchant**.

## Recommended next step

Run a field-validation pilot before allocating acquisition budget. Measure active acceptance, merchant categories, competitor coverage, onboarding eligibility, travel effort, and acquisition cost. Evaluate 30-day active merchants and incremental transactions against comparable areas rather than counting registrations alone.

## Limits

PhonePe Pulse covers the PhonePe ecosystem rather than the entire UPI market. Registered users and merchants are cumulative registrations, not active participants. District transaction totals include all payment categories, including P2P. The public data does not contain the local shop universe, active acceptance, competition, acquisition cost, revenue, or realized pilot outcomes. The score prioritizes research; it does not prove causation or forecast ROI.
